# Outreach And Workbench Handoff

Read this reference only when the user asks to generate outreach, import results, or update a workbench/CRM after prospecting.

## Authorization And Data Safety

Finding prospects and creating a local result do not authorize external writes or communication. Before sending, uploading, or changing live status, confirm that the user requested that specific action and follow any action-time confirmation required by the environment.

Deduplicate again against the destination immediately before import. On duplicates, enrich missing factual fields only; preserve contact status, messages, replies, manual notes, owner, and follow-up dates. Report inserted, duplicate, enriched, skipped, and failed counts.

## Personalization Signal Selection

Extract all verified candidate signals before writing and choose one primary signal by this order:

1. recent professional post or event;
2. new role or promotion;
3. current responsibility;
4. current project;
5. company strategy;
6. relevant work experience.

Use education only when directly relevant to the current product application. Do not expose internal interaction counts, scores, authority estimates, or research notes. If deleting the name and company leaves a message suitable for most prospects, regenerate it with a more specific verified connection.

## LinkedIn Invitation

Goal: earn connection acceptance, not sell.

- 180-260 English characters; never exceed 300.
- Use one verified trigger or responsibility.
- Connect it to the person's business value and the sender's relevant industry identity.
- End with a low-pressure connection request.
- Do not request a meeting, quotation, procurement list, or catalogue review.

Structure:

```text
specific signal -> role/business connection -> relevant identity -> light connection request
```

## InMail Or Cold Email

Goal: earn a reply, not complete the sale.

- Subject: 3-7 specific English words. Avoid `question`, `opportunity`, `synergy`, and `cooperation` as empty hooks.
- Body: 80-130 English words, `Hi + first name`, natural first/second person, mostly sentences under 22 words.
- First paragraph: one verified signal or current responsibility; never recite the full resume.
- Second paragraph: one honest business hypothesis explaining possible relevance without claiming active demand.
- Third paragraph: the most relevant 1-3 products/capabilities and one credible proof point.
- End with one easy-to-answer question.

Do not use template phrases such as `Your background includes`, `showcasing their expertise`, or `explore synergies`. Do not stack three or more abstract business terms.

## First Message After Connection

Goal: make the sender understandable and create a concrete reason to reply.

- 65-90 English words.
- Brief identity and relevant product/capability.
- One credible proof point.
- Translate that capability into the recipient role's likely business value.
- End with one permission-based question, such as whether a short relevant comparison or catalogue section would be useful.

Invitation and InMail happen before connection and do not count as formal conversation rounds.

## Seen But No Reply

Do not repeat the introduction, full product list, catalogue offer, or generic relevance question. Add one new role-specific value hook supported by known facts, such as application matching, batch consistency, wear-life comparison, installed-equipment compatibility, tender documentation, channel coverage, or supply-risk comparison. Ask one small diagnostic question.

After the customer replies, answer the customer's actual words before advancing the sales process. Base follow-up on the conversation record, not a fixed template.

## Claim Limits

Never invent demand, purchasing authority, projects, customers, certifications, results, or delivery capability. Without factory confirmation, do not promise nonstandard customization, expedited supply, fixed warranty terms, unconditional returns, freight coverage, or overseas service conditions.
